## Kolkata Manipal Hospital Mukundapur

[Manipal Hospital Mukundapur](https://medifyr.com/kolkata/hospital/manipal-hospital-mukundapur) is a multi-speciality hospital in Kolkata offering medical and surgical services across a range of clinical specialities. The hospital, formerly known as AMRI Hospital Mukundapur, is a well-established healthcare institution for patients looking for specialist consultations, diagnostic services, inpatient treatment and surgical care in Kolkata. The hospital was established in 2015 and has a capacity of 500 beds and is NABH accredited.

The hospital has specialists in many different branches of medicine so patients can get many different kinds of health care in one place. Its clinical departments are Cardiac Surgery, Neurosurgery, Orthopaedics, Nephrology, Gastroenterology and General Surgery. Besides these great specialities, the hospital gives care in many other fields of medicine and surgery.

### Medical Specialities & Services

Manipal Hospital Mukundapur has expertise in a wide range of specialities. Patients can consult with specialists in cardiology, cardiac surgery, neurology, neurosurgery, orthopaedics, nephrology, gastroenterology, urology, oncology and internal medicine. The hospital also has departments related to paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology, pulmonology, ENT, dermatology, ophthalmology and other areas of healthcare also.

A diverse set of specialities can facilitate a coordinated approach to diagnosing and treating patients with complex medical needs. The hospital also has specialities in critical care, haematology, psychiatry, rheumatology, vascular surgery and a variety of paediatric specialities.

### Treatment Procedures

Manipal Hospital Mukundapur provides a variety of medical procedures and treatments. These include Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG), Bone Marrow Transplant, Brain Tumour Surgery and Knee Replacement. The hospital also provides advanced treatments like Transarterial Radioembolisation (TARE), Transarterial Chemoembolisation (TACE), organ transplant-related procedures and treatments for blood and liver cancer, etc.

Patients should consult the relevant specialist to determine if a specific procedure or treatment is suitable for their individual medical condition.

### Facilities and Care of Patients

The hospital has a number of key facilities to assist patients during consultations, treatment and stays in the hospital. It offers amenities such as emergency services, inpatient and outpatient care, radiology and imaging services, intensive care units (ICU), housekeeping and maintenance support.

The emergency department handles urgent medical needs while outpatient services provide access to specialist consultations for scheduled medical care. Doctors also rely on diagnostic and imaging services to evaluate different health conditions and create appropriate treatment regimens.
